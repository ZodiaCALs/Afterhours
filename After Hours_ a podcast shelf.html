夜更かしラジオ

After hours: a podcast shelf for slow evenings. City pop, jazz, piano, gadgets, books, mysteries and big ideas, sorted by mood.

Start with these five: Composer of the Week Primer In Our Time Shedunnit Mindscape

City pop and anime songs

Neon, slap bass, and the story behind the songs.

  • PrimerJapanese city pop, one album per episode.
  • Mayonaka Hour 真夜中アワーProfiles of city pop’s big names, from Yamashita to Matsubara.
  • Liner Notes: Pacific BreezeLight in the Attic’s companion to the Pacific Breeze compilations.
  • Anisongs and the History of Anime MusicA one-off Japan Society talk covering the 1960s to now.
  • Anime InstrumentalityOP/ED and soundtrack reviews, including an older piano episode.

Jazz, hip-hop and piano

Late-night crate-digging and quiet keys.

  • Jazz Night in AmericaLive jazz from NPR and WBGO.
  • Heat RocksGuests on one favorite album: jazz, hip-hop, soul. About 200 episodes in the archive.
  • Take It PersonalGolden-era, boom-bap East Coast hip-hop.
  • The Bridge: 50 Years of Hip-HopNas and Miss Info, a Spotify original. Info page; check Spotify for new episodes.
  • Composer of the WeekBBC Radio 3 portraits of composers. Podcast feed is UK only.
  • Song ExploderArtists take their own songs apart.

Gadgets and latest tech

News and devices, from easygoing to after-midnight.

  • The VergecastWeekly gadget and tech news.
  • UpgradeRelaxed Apple and tech conversation.
  • Hard ForkAI and tech news from the NYT.
  • Darknet DiariesTrue stories from the security world.
  • Rebuild.fm日本語Long-running Japanese tech talk.

Literature and light lit

Books, ideas, and conversation in English, Japanese and Chinese.

  • In Our TimeBBC: literature, science, history and philosophy with academics.
  • BacklistedForgotten and classic books, warmly discussed.
  • The New Yorker: FictionAuthors read and discuss short stories.
  • 随机波动中文Culture, books and society, with scholars as guests.
  • ゆる言語学ラジオ日本語Light, funny linguistics. Sister shows cover astronomy and music.

Mystery novels

Golden Age puzzles and modern whodunits.

  • ShedunnitThe history of Golden Age detective fiction.
  • I Hear of Sherlock EverywhereSherlock Holmes, in depth.
  • 波罗咖啡馆中文Chinese mystery chat; covers Japanese mystery writers such as Uketsu.

Economics, physics and astronomy

Big ideas, explained gently.

  • Planet MoneyFriendly storytelling about how money works.
  • The IndicatorShort daily economics.
  • EconTalkLong, thoughtful interviews.
  • Odd LotsMarkets and finance, with a curious streak.
  • Sean Carroll’s MindscapePhysics and big questions.
  • The Infinite Monkey CageBBC science with comedians.
  • RadiolabAtmospheric science storytelling.

Game industry

Where games came from and how they get made.

  • RetronautsRetro games, with a lot of Japanese game history.
  • Video Game History HourDevelopers and historians, every other week.

Heat Rocks finished its Maximum Fun run in 2021; the archive is still worth a listen.

Composer of the Week’s podcast feed is UK only. Check each show’s page for its current schedule.
